My IGIBA R8 Gaming Mouse

Lightweight • Fast • Fully Modded

About the Mouse

The IGIBA R8 Wired Gaming Mouse is an affordable gaming mouse that delivers solid performance for casual and competitive gaming. It has responsive buttons, RGB lighting, and a comfortable shape that works well for long gaming sessions.

For the price, it performs surprisingly well and is a great mouse for people who enjoy upgrading and modding their gaming gear.

My Mods

🔧 Weight Reduction Mod

I opened the mouse and removed the internal weight. This made the mouse much lighter and easier to flick quickly in fast-paced games. The reduced weight helps improve reaction speed and reduces hand fatigue.

🎯 Universal Mouse Skates

I replaced the stock skates with universal mouse skates. These glide much smoother across my mousepad and improve tracking consistency.

⭕ Mouse Ring Skate

I added a mouse ring skate around the sensor area for even smoother movement. This helps the mouse feel faster and more controlled.

✋ Grip Tape

I installed grip tape on the sides and buttons. This gives me better control during intense gaming sessions and prevents slipping when my hands get sweaty.

Final Verdict

⭐ Rating: 8.5/10

After all the mods, my IGIBA R8 feels significantly better than stock. The lighter weight, smoother skates, ring skate, and grip tape make it faster, more comfortable, and more competitive for FPS and aiming games.

It's proof that even a budget gaming mouse can become a great performer with the right modifications.
